03 / Experiential Marketing

EXPERIENCE PROPOSAL & ACUMEN

The Blocky Odyssey

An experiential marketing proposal and cross-platform framework introducing a story-driven, interactive Minecraft attraction to Tokyo DisneySea. Designed around extensive consumer demographic evaluations, this campaign flips passive rider behaviors into active gamified conversion funnels.

  • Target Segment: Urban Young Women aged 18–29 in the Greater Tokyo Area prioritizing shareable, aesthetic moments.
  • Omnichannel Activation: AIDA promotional design blending stop-motion Tokyo subway immersive tunnels with active in-park Explorer Collection Quests.
  • Conversion Engine: A LINE-based closed funnel ecosystem driving ticket acquisition, maps navigation, and phygital souvenir monetization channels.
✦ Strategic Framework: Integrated OLC Group Metrics & Gamification Models
